About

Malaysian Cellist and Recording Artist.

Available for bookings

Mariamlisa holds a Bachelor's Degree in Music from Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), with formal training in cello and piano, in classical and contemporary music.

She performs at live concerts, television productions, studio sessions, weddings and corporate events, alongside established Malaysian and international musicians.
