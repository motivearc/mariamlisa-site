Mariamlisa, Cellist

About

Malaysian cellist and recording artist, trained in classical and contemporary music.

Mariamlisa holds a Bachelor's Degree in Music from Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), with formal training in cello and piano.

She performs at live concerts, television productions, studio sessions, weddings and corporate events, alongside established Malaysian and international musicians.
